## Research outcomes and exclusions

- **iFlight (4):** The UK-localised iFlight Europe storefront presented GBP prices and stated UK VAT/duties are included with no import fees, but it also explicitly stated products ship from Austria. Under the current domestic UK seller threshold, this pass did not classify these as UK domestic retailer observations. The Nazgul XL5 results also showed differing GBP prices across captured variants/pages, so no price was chosen.
- **Parrot ANAFI FPV:** Currys Business lists product code PF728050 but unavailable; Drones Direct marks the PF728050 FPV package no longer available. No current price imported.
- **Parrot ANAFI USA GOV/MIL:** Enterprise UAV's £7,800 listing is the standard ANAFI USA package, not the exact GOV or MIL pack. Parrot's official pack selector identifies distinct GOV/MIL controller and package contents but provides no public UK GBP price.
- **Parrot ANAFI UKR / GOV / XLR:** UK dealer and Parrot sources found were quote-only or technical pages. Coptrz prices were for drone service, not the aircraft. The Leicester Drones £1,359 listing is the XLR battery alone, not the aircraft pack.
- **Legacy Parrot products:** Mambo Fly marketplace results were active listings rather than completed sales. Jumping Night Diesel is not Jumping Night Marshall. Hydrofoil New Z aggregator results were not a current direct retailer offer. No exact UK price was confirmed for the other legacy models.
- **Potensic A20:** OnBuy displayed a cached/expired listing with old delivery dates and no sufficiently current confirmation; not imported.
- **Potensic A20 Mini:** BargainFox's three-battery offer was sold out and package-mismatched to the Standard Package master.
- **Potensic A30:** No exact current direct UK source was found.
- No used candidate qualified as a direct UK completed sale with exact model/package and reliable seller.
