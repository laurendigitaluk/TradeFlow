# Original Nintendo Switch controller catalogue — Batch 17

Date: 5 November 2026
Repository: laurendigitaluk/TradeFlow (main)

## Additions
Eight CRKD ATOM Collectible Keychain Controller colour variants were added independently to LIVE and TEST. Each has active UK-new GBP Selfridges evidence at £17.99 from the colour-selectable UK listing:
https://www.selfridges.com/GB/en/product/crkd-atom-keychain-nintendo-switch-controller_R04386317/

CRKD official product page: https://crkd.gg/products/crkd-atom
Official compatibility: Nintendo Switch, PC/Mac, mobile/tablet and Smart TV; Bluetooth wireless; up to 10 hours play time; four shoulder buttons; wrist strap included.

Selfridges groups eight colours in one listing. Manufacturer colour names used in the catalogue:
Atomic Purple, Sky Blue, Glacier Blue, CRKD Grey, Leaf Green, Hot Pink, PAL Grey, Yuzu Yellow.
Selfridges selector labels Blue/Grey/Pink map to the manufacturer names Sky Blue/CRKD Grey/Hot Pink.

CRKD's documentation lists three additional colours (OG Black, OG Grey, Retro Purple). They were not imported because the current UK retailer listing does not show them among its eight choices. Research reliable UK GBP evidence before adding those variants. ATOM Keychain is distinct from ATOM+ Mini.
