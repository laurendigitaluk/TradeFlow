  1. Choose a template.
  2. Enter your business identity and main message.
  3. Set up your buying categories. These categories also define the selling category used when products are added to inventory.
  4. Add products in Inventory and assign the correct category and properties.
  5. Complete every website page below, especially Contact, About, Terms and Privacy.
  6. Preview everything, save the draft, then publish.

Step 3 · Business identity
Buying categories and selling products

First create your categories. Go to Categories & Properties. A category such as DJI Drones, Cameras or Power Tools can be enabled for Buying and Selling.

When Buying is enabled, customers can use that category when submitting items to you. When the item is acquired and added to Inventory, the same category is used for the product. If Selling is enabled, that product category can then be used for your shop/listings. You do not need to build a separate duplicate category just to sell the item.

Use Inventory to add the actual products. Use category properties for things such as model, condition, colour, storage, serial number or other information that belongs to that type of product.
