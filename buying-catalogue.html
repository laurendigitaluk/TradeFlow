Buying Pricing & Master Catalogue

This is the single place to decide what your business buys and how it buys it. Products come from TradeFlow's independent master catalogue. The catalogue is organised as Manufacturer → Category → Branch → Model → Package / configuration. Each package/configuration is a separate buying product, so different controllers, batteries or bundles can be priced independently.

Pricing controls the buying catalogue. Choose the category, branch and manufacturer you actually buy, then select only the model/package configurations you want in your Buying Catalogue. TradeFlow loads products on demand rather than downloading the full master catalogue. Selecting a product creates/links its category, branch and manufacturer automatically. After selection, use Manual valuation, Manual price / override or Automatic pricing. Off removes the product from active Buying and clears its buying price configuration.
