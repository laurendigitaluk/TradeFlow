How connection works

Connect your domain to TradeFlow

  1. Enter your domain below. Use your own domain, such as example.co.uk or www.example.co.uk.
  2. TradeFlow prepares the connection. Your domain is saved against your business and the required DNS connection details will be provided for it.
  3. Open Porkbun. Go to Domain Management, select your domain, then open DNS / DNS Records. Porkbun allows A, CNAME and TXT records to be added there.
  4. Add the exact DNS records TradeFlow gives you. Do not guess the target or replace existing email records unless TradeFlow specifically tells you to.
  5. Wait for DNS and SSL validation. Cloudflare then verifies the custom hostname and provisions the website certificate.
  6. TradeFlow activates the domain. Once verified, your published website will respond on your own domain.

Porkbun is the registrar/DNS provider. Cloudflare handles the secure website connection and SSL for TradeFlow customer domains. The exact DNS target must come from the TradeFlow connection service; it is not safe to invent one.
