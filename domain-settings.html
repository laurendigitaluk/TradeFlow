I already have my domain
Before you connect it

1. Buy your domain

  1. Search for the exact domain name and extension you want.
  2. Check the first-year price and the normal renewal price.
  3. Buy the domain through your own registrar account.
  4. Confirm that the domain appears in the registrar's domain-management area.

2. Return to TradeFlow

  1. Enter your domain below.
  2. Click Save website URL.
  3. TradeFlow creates a connection request for the Platform Owner.
  4. The Platform Owner prepares the TradeFlow connection and provides the DNS instructions you need.
  5. Sign in to your registrar and add the exact DNS records supplied by TradeFlow.
  6. Do not guess DNS targets or remove existing email records unless TradeFlow specifically tells you to.
  7. Wait for DNS and secure website verification.
Important: TradeFlow will never ask for your registrar password. Your domain remains in your registrar account and you remain responsible for its renewal.

How connection works

One website, one address

  1. Add your domain above.
  2. Point the domain's DNS to the TradeFlow website hosting target provided during the hosting setup.
  3. TradeFlow verifies the domain and marks it active.
  4. When your website is published, the published-site index maps that hostname to your business website automatically.

The DNS/hosting target is intentionally not hard-coded yet because the multi-subscriber hosting layer has not been selected. We should not invent a DNS target and risk sending subscriber domains to the wrong service.
