How the website connects to TradeFlow
  1. Choose a template. It changes the visual starting layout, not your TradeFlow data.
  2. Enter your business name, headline, introduction and brand colour.
  3. Build your Buying Categories in Categories & Properties.
  4. When a category is enabled for Buying and Selling, the same category can be used for both sides of the business. You do not create a duplicate selling category.
  5. Add the actual stock in Inventory and assign the category and product properties.
  6. Use Selling to control which products become customer-facing listings.
  7. Complete the website pages below, preview them, save the draft and then publish.

Buying categories create the structure for your products

Start with Categories & Properties. For example: DJI Drones, Cameras, Lenses or Power Tools.

Enable Buying when customers can submit that type of item to you. Enable Selling when products in that category can be sold through the shop. The database uses one category with Buying/Selling flags; it does not create a second duplicate category.

Then add category properties such as model, condition, colour, storage, serial number or other information that applies to that product type. Add stock in Inventory and assign the category and properties.
