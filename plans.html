TradeFlow Basic and Enhanced are built around the same core Buy & Sell operation. Basic gives you the tools to run the complete business; Enhanced adds the tools for teams, oversight, integrations, analytics and market intelligence.

How the plans work

Basic is the complete starting point for a Buy & Sell business. It covers buying, valuation, trade-ins, inventory, selling, customer orders, fulfilment and your own customer-facing website.

Enhanced includes everything in Basic and adds capabilities aimed at businesses with staff, higher operational complexity and a greater need for reporting, integrations and market intelligence.

THE COMPLETE CORE WORKSPACE

Basic

Everything needed to run the day-to-day operation of a Buy & Sell business.

Buying

Manage the buying side of your business, including customer requests and the process of bringing goods into your operation.

Valuation

Use manual valuations and rule-based valuation methods to help assess items before acquisition.

Trade-ins & offers

Handle trade-in activity and offers as part of the buying and selling workflow.

Inventory management

Keep a controlled product record as an item moves through receipt, condition, photographs, preparation and its journey towards sale.

Selling

Create and manage listings for inventory that is ready to sell through your customer-facing sales channels.

Orders

Manage customer orders and keep the sale connected to the product and business workflow.

Fulfilment

Move orders through fulfilment, including delivery and tracking information where required.

Customer portal

Give customers their own account area where they can manage their buying, selling, orders and other customer activity.

Customer-facing website

Give your business its own branded website so customers can discover products and interact with your business.

Website Builder

Configure the presentation of your customer website with templates, colours, your logo, images, custom categories and custom subcategories.

Website preview & publishing

Preview changes before publishing and control when your customer-facing website is made live.

FOR GROWING OPERATIONS

Enhanced

Everything in Basic, plus the tools that become useful when a business has staff, more activity and greater operational needs.

Everything in Basic

Enhanced does not remove the core Buy & Sell operation. It includes the complete Basic workspace and adds the capabilities below.

Staff management

Manage additional staff members within the business workspace. The current plan configuration allows up to 10 staff.

Staff messaging

Provide internal communication tools so members of the team can communicate within the business workspace.

Audit tools

Gain additional visibility over activity and changes within the business, supporting accountability and operational oversight.

Analytics

Access additional reporting and business-performance information to help understand activity as the operation grows.

Integrations

Connect TradeFlow with external systems and services where integrations are available and appropriate for the business.

Market intelligence

Use additional market-information capabilities designed to support businesses that need more insight into pricing and market activity.

What the difference means

BasicRun your complete Buy & Sell business.
EnhancedRun and grow a more involved operation with staff, oversight, analytics, integrations and market intelligence.

TradeFlow plan features may evolve as the platform develops. Any usage limits, payment fees, integration availability or future feature restrictions will be stated separately when applicable.
