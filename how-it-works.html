Your TradeFlow subscriptionOne complete plan for your business

TradeFlow is offered as one complete platform plan. It brings together your business website, product catalogue, buying and valuation tools, inventory, retail listings, customer accounts, orders and fulfilment.

The current plan includes a 30-day free trial, followed by £59.99 per month. Your payment method is collected securely through Stripe when you sign up. Monthly billing starts automatically after the trial unless you cancel beforehand.

Your TradeFlow subscription is separate from the payment account your business connects for taking payments from its own retail customers.

Your business website templateA ready-made starting point for your own website

Your business starts with a ready-made TradeFlow website template. It gives you a structured starting point, rather than asking you to build a website from a blank page.

You can make the website your own by adding your business name, logo, colours, wording, photographs, product categories and the information customers need before buying or selling with you.

Your public website is the front door to your business. Customers can browse what you buy and sell, submit valuation requests, create an account where required and shop from your published retail listings.

Using the website builderEdit your pages and publish your website

The Website Builder lets you edit the content and appearance of your business website. You can update text, headings, images, buttons, page sections, backgrounds and colours using the available editing controls.

  • Add your business details, logo and brand colours.
  • Edit page headings, descriptions and other website text.
  • Choose and arrange the sections shown on your pages.
  • Set up the What We Buy and What We Sell areas.
  • Preview your changes before publishing them.

Your edits remain a draft until you publish. Publishing makes the approved version visible on your customer-facing website. Your website content and your private TradeFlow business workspace are separate parts of the platform.

Choosing products from the catalogueCentral AI research, verified UK prices and source links

TradeFlow includes a central product catalogue covering a wide range of manufacturers, product types and models. Your business can search the catalogue and choose the products relevant to what you buy and sell.

TradeFlow's central AI Research Centre coordinates specialist Gemma research agents across category-approved UK websites. The agents work through selected product batches, check exact manufacturer and model matches, and gather UK new and used price evidence with source links.

Research candidates are checked before they are added to the master catalogue. ChatGPT and the Platform Owner review the source, product match, price and condition; a discovery is not automatically approved or imported. Subscribers use this centrally operated research capability and do not need to connect their own AI provider.

UK new-market evidence can support automatic valuation guidance where the product and evidence are available. UK used-market observations are retained as reference information and do not directly determine automatic valuations. Your business remains responsible for reviewing the item and deciding the offer it is prepared to make.

The master catalogue is separate from the products you choose to show on your own website. Selecting a catalogue product does not automatically publish it as a retail listing.

Connecting your payment methodsSet up payments for your own retail customers

Your business connects its payment account through the secure payment setup provided by Stripe. Follow the connection and verification steps, then add or confirm the business and bank details requested by Stripe.

Once the payment account is connected and enabled, your business can accept supported online payments from customers placing retail orders through your shop. Card details are handled by Stripe's secure checkout and payment services; TradeFlow does not store customers' full card details.

There are two separate payment arrangements:

  • TradeFlow subscription: your monthly platform subscription is billed separately.
  • Your retail sales: payments from customers buying products from your business are processed through your connected payment account.

Payments you make to people selling items to your business are a different part of the buying process. TradeFlow records the payment and purchase progress; it does not automatically send customer payouts.

How the valuation system worksFrom a customer's product submission to your offer

A customer visits your website, chooses the product they want to sell, enters the requested details and condition, and uploads photographs. The valuation request is then sent into the Buying area of your TradeFlow workspace.

Where supported, the selected catalogue product and available UK new-market research can provide automatic valuation guidance. Your business can review the submission, photographs, condition and available evidence before deciding what to offer.

You can prepare a cash offer or a trade-in offer and send it to the customer. The customer can accept the relevant option or decline. For requests containing more than one item, decisions can be handled for the individual items where the workflow allows.

After an offer is accepted, your business arranges the shipping handoff. When the item arrives, you confirm receipt and inspect it against the customer's description. You can confirm the original offer, send a revised offer for the customer to accept or decline, or refuse the purchase where appropriate.

If the inspection confirms the original offer, the customer does not need to accept the same offer a second time. A revised offer is sent back to the customer for a fresh decision.

Customer payments and completing a purchaseRecording payment and moving the item into stock

Once the inspection and offer decision are complete, your business makes the agreed payment to the customer using the agreed payment method, such as bank transfer or cash where appropriate.

You then record the payment in TradeFlow and complete the purchase. TradeFlow keeps the buying record and customer progress together, but it does not automatically transfer the purchase money to the customer.

Payment completion is the point at which the purchased item moves into Inventory. Accepting an offer on its own does not complete the purchase or move the item into stock.

Postage, labels and trackingThe customer pays the carrier directly; TradeFlow does not handle shipping costs

In your business settings, open Shipping Settings and select the shipping services you use. These saved services are then available when you arrange the shipping handoff for a buying request.

Your business selects the shipping services it supports. The customer pays the chosen carrier directly using that carrier's own website or service. TradeFlow does not book couriers, create courier quotes, collect shipping charges or pay courier costs.

Your business provides the customer with the shipping instructions and, where required, a label or QR code. The customer adds the tracking number and shipping details, uses the selected carrier service and confirms when the item has been handed over. Your business then confirms when the item physically arrives and moves it on to inspection.

For retail orders, your business manages the shipping information and fulfilment for the order. Customers can follow the order and tracking progress through their account where those details have been provided.

Inventory, retail listings and your shopTurn completed purchases into products customers can buy

When a purchase is completed, the item moves into your Inventory. Its product details remain connected to the buying record, giving your business one place to prepare the item for resale.

From Inventory, your business can check the item details, add or review photographs, record its condition, set a selling price and prepare the retail description. You can then create a listing and publish it to your customer-facing shop, using the available sales-channel options for your business.

When a customer buys the product, the order moves into your TradeFlow sales and fulfilment workflow. Your business manages the order, shipping and tracking. The listing's availability is updated through the sales process so that a sold item is not offered as available stock.

In short: Buying → completed purchase → Inventory → listing → your shop → customer order → fulfilment.

Managing your business day to dayYour main business activity in one workspace

Your TradeFlow dashboard brings the main parts of your operation together. From the workspace, your business can move between Buying, Inventory, Selling, Orders, Fulfilment, Customers, Website and Settings.

Use the dashboard to see where work is waiting, review customer requests, manage stock, prepare listings, follow retail orders and complete fulfilment. Customer accounts provide customers with their own view of relevant valuation requests, offers, orders and progress.

TradeFlow connects these activities so your business can follow an item from the first customer enquiry through purchase and into resale without treating each stage as a separate system.
