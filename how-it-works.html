THE TRADEFLOW PROCESS

From valuation to sale.

One clear journey for the customer. One connected workflow for the business.

PART 1 · CUSTOMER BUYING JOURNEY

Seven stages to complete a purchase

The customer follows progress in their account while the subscriber manages each business action in Buying.

  1. 01ValuationItem details and photos submitted; business reviews and values.
  2. 02OfferCustomer accepts cash, trade-in, or declines.
  3. 03ShippingBusiness supplies label or QR code and instructions.
  4. 04ReceivedCustomer confirms dispatch; business confirms arrival.
  5. 05InspectionBusiness checks the item and records its condition.
  6. 06PaymentBusiness records payment against the agreed offer.
  7. 07CompletePurchase is completed and the item moves into stock.
WHO DOES WHAT

Customer and business actions

Customer

  1. Submit item details, condition and photographs.
  2. Choose cash, trade-in or decline the offer.
  3. Use the supplied shipping label and confirm dispatch.
  4. Review and respond only if a revised offer is sent.
  5. Follow payment and completion status in the account.

Subscriber business

  1. Review the request and send cash or trade-in values.
  2. Arrange shipping with the chosen carrier outside TradeFlow.
  3. Upload the label or QR code, tracking details and instructions.
  4. Confirm receipt, inspect the item and accept, revise or refuse.
  5. Record payment and complete the purchase.

Important: TradeFlow records the shipping handoff; it does not book couriers or collect shipping payments. The subscriber records payment; TradeFlow does not automatically pay the customer. If the original offer is unchanged after inspection, the customer does not need to accept it again.

PART 2 · AFTER THE PURCHASE

Purchased item → retail sale

The completed purchase becomes business stock. The subscriber then prepares and publishes the retail listing.

  1. InventoryPurchased item and inspection details are retained.
  2. Prepare listingCheck details, condition, photographs and asking price.
  3. Publish to shopMake the listing available on the subscriber website.
  4. Sale & fulfilmentComplete the customer order and dispatch.
