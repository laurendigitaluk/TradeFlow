In-Store Valuation

Use the same TradeFlow catalogue and valuation engine as the online selling journey while the customer is physically with you. This is the dedicated in-store counter screen; it does not use the public customer selling journey. The item is checked at the counter and, if accepted, moves directly to Inventory without shipping or a later inspection.

1. CustomerRecord the customer and ID check
2. ItemFollow the same catalogue selections
3. ValuationUse the existing TradeFlow valuation engine
4. PurchaseAccept and add directly to Inventory

Customer

No customer TradeFlow account is required for an in-store transaction.

Customer identity & right to sell

Record the business's identity check before calculating or completing the purchase.
The customer must confirm that they have the legal right to sell the item. The ID check is recorded as part of the business's in-store transaction record.

Item & condition

These selections mirror the online selling journey. Choose the item step by step so the same catalogue product and valuation rules are used.

Calculate Valuation

This calls the existing TradeFlow buying valuation engine. It does not create a separate in-store pricing system.
Manual in-store offer

Use this only when the automatic valuation engine reports that the product does not yet have the required pricing evidence or condition rule.
