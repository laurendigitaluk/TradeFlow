How connection works

Connect your domain to TradeFlow

  1. Enter your domain below. Use your own domain, such as example.co.uk or www.example.co.uk.
  2. TradeFlow sends the connection request to the Platform Owner. The owner prepares the approved hosting connection and exact DNS instructions.
  3. Apply the exact DNS instructions. Use your registrar DNS controls. Do not guess or replace existing email records unless TradeFlow specifically tells you to.
  4. TradeFlow verifies DNS, SSL and routing. Your domain remains pending until all three are confirmed.
  5. Your domain becomes active. Your published subscriber website then responds on your own domain.

You keep ownership of the domain, registrar account, renewal and billing. TradeFlow only manages the connection to your subscriber website.
