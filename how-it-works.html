THE PROCESS, STEP BY STEP

From the first item details to the final decision.

Here is how the customer and subscriber dashboards work together at each point.

01
CUSTOMER SUBMISSION

Choose the item and tell the business about it.

The customer selects a category and product, then supplies the manufacturer, model, package or version, condition, missing items and any additional description. Photographs can be added to help the business review the item.

TradeFlow records / next step: TradeFlow records the submitted valuation request and its item details for the subscriber to review.
02
SUBSCRIBER VALUATION

Review the details and prepare the offer.

The subscriber opens the request in Buying, checks the supplied information and available valuation evidence, and enters the cash offer and trade-in offer before sending the valuation to the customer.

TradeFlow records / next step: The valuation and offer are linked to the submitted item.
03
CUSTOMER OFFER DECISION

The customer chooses how to proceed.

The customer can accept the cash offer, accept the trade-in offer or refuse the item. For a request containing several items, the customer can make decisions item by item or choose an available combined offer for the request.

TradeFlow records / next step: Accepted items move to the shipping handoff. Refused items do not proceed as purchases.
04
SHIPPING HANDOFF

The business supplies the shipping instructions.

The subscriber chooses one of their saved shipping services and arranges the shipment directly with that provider, outside TradeFlow. They return to Buying and provide the customer with the label or QR code, carrier details, tracking information and instructions.

TradeFlow records / next step: TradeFlow manages the handoff information; it does not book the courier or process the courier's shipping payment.
05
CUSTOMER DISPATCH AND RECEIPT

The customer sends it; the business confirms arrival.

The customer opens or prints the supplied label or QR code, packages the item and hands it to the carrier. They confirm in their account when it has been sent. When the parcel physically arrives, the subscriber confirms receipt in Buying.

TradeFlow records / next step: Dispatch confirmation and subscriber receipt confirmation are recorded as separate events.
06
PHYSICAL INSPECTION

Check the item and decide the outcome.

The subscriber compares the physical item with the customer's description and records the inspected condition. They can accept the original offer, make a counteroffer where appropriate, or refuse the purchase if the inspection outcome requires it.

TradeFlow records / next step: The inspection decision determines the next purchase stage.
07
REVISED FINAL OFFER, IF NEEDED

Give the customer a chance to respond to a changed value.

If inspection changes the agreed value, the subscriber can send a revised final offer. The customer can accept or reject that offer. If the original accepted value remains unchanged, the purchase can continue on that original offer.

TradeFlow records / next step: The original offer and any revised final offer remain distinct in the purchase history.
08
PAYMENT AND COMPLETION

Record payment and complete the purchase.

The subscriber records the payment method and reference, confirms that payment has been sent and completes the purchase. The customer can see the payment and completed status in their account.

TradeFlow records / next step: The purchased item is created as an inventory asset for the business.
AFTER THE PURCHASE

Buying is only the beginning.

Once the purchase is complete, TradeFlow carries the item into the business's stock workflow. The subscriber can prepare a retail listing without starting the product record again.

01▣

Inventory

The purchased item becomes an inventory asset with its purchase, inspection and photograph records retained.

02✎

Prepare the listing

The subscriber reviews the pre-filled product details, condition and photographs, then adds the asking price and retail information.

03⌂

Publish to the shop

The completed listing is published to the subscriber's customer-facing website and becomes available to browse.

04✓

Sale and fulfilment

When sold, the item is no longer available to another buyer and moves through the order and dispatch process.
