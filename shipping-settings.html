Shipping integrations

Connect your shipping services

Business-wide

Connect Parcel2Go here. TradeFlow uses your Parcel2Go account as its single integrated shipping connection. Customers never enter courier credentials and TradeFlow does not collect, pay or reimburse shipping costs.

After Parcel2Go is connected and tested, it appears under Send shipping label → Use integrated shipping in Buying. Manual label and QR-code handoff remains available separately.

How it works

From connection to customer label

1. Connect Parcel2Go here
Connect your Parcel2Go account and save the business connection securely.
2. Choose the courier in Buying
Open an accepted buying request and choose Use integrated shipping. Parcel2Go provides the available courier services for the shipment.
3. Customer receives the handoff
TradeFlow sends the label, QR code or shipping instructions to the customer. The customer does not configure the courier account.
