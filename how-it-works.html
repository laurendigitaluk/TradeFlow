WHO DOES WHAT

Customer and business actions

Customer

  1. Submit item details, condition and photographs.
  2. Choose cash, trade-in or decline the offer.
  3. Use the supplied shipping label and confirm dispatch.
  4. Review and respond if a revised offer is sent.
  5. Follow payment and completion status in the account.

Subscriber business

  1. Review the request and send cash or trade-in values.
  2. Arrange shipping with the chosen carrier outside TradeFlow.
  3. Upload the label or QR code, tracking details and instructions.
  4. Confirm receipt, inspect the item and accept, revise or refuse.
  5. Record payment and complete the purchase.
Important: Shipping is arranged by the subscriber outside TradeFlow. TradeFlow does not book couriers or collect the customer’s shipping payment. Payment to the customer is made by the subscriber and recorded in TradeFlow; it is not an automatic payout. If inspection confirms the original offer, the customer does not need to accept it again. Any revised offer must be presented for the customer to accept or decline.
