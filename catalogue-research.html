MARKET RESEARCH

Evidence is researched, checked and refreshed

The catalogue is more than a list of product names. Research is attached to the relevant product so your business can see where market information came from and when it was observed.

01

Identify the exact product

Match the manufacturer, model, generation, storage or configuration, and package or bundle. Similar names are checked so evidence is not attached to the wrong variant.

02

Research the UK market

Find relevant UK retailer and marketplace references. Product identity, price, condition, availability and the type of listing are considered together.

03

Check and compare evidence

Review source relevance, remove duplicate or mismatched records, and keep separate products and bundles distinct where they represent different items.

04

Record the source details

Evidence can retain the source name and link, observed price in GBP, observation date, condition, sales channel, availability and research notes.

05

Continue refreshing the catalogue

As research progresses, new products are added and existing evidence can be revisited. The aim is an expanding, traceable reference base rather than a fixed price list.

NEW-MARKET EVIDENCE

Can support valuation guidance

Where the product and suitable UK new-market evidence are available, that evidence can support TradeFlow's automatic valuation guidance.

USED-MARKET EVIDENCE

Kept as reference information

Used-market observations help provide context, but they do not directly determine the automatic buying valuation.

YOUR BUSINESS DECIDES

Research does not make the offer

Your business reviews the customer's item, condition and details, then decides the offer it is prepared to make. Research is supporting information, not a substitute for that decision.
