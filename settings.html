Shipping integrations

Shipping services

Subscriber account

Set up your shipping services here before the website goes live. This is the subscriber's business shipping setup. This is the subscriber business setup, not a customer setting. Once connected, the Buying and Acquisition workspaces will show the connected service for each accepted sale, and the same provider connection can be used for quotes, labels and tracking as the integration supports them.

You can connect the shipping providers your business uses. Each provider below has setup instructions. Once a provider is connected and tested, it appears in the Send shipping label → Use integrated shipping list. Customers never enter provider API credentials. Customers pay the shipping provider directly; TradeFlow does not collect, pay or reimburse shipping costs.
