THE TRADEFLOW PROCESS

From valuation to sale.

Follow the customer journey from first valuation through to purchase, inventory and the final retail sale. The screenshots below show the real TradeFlow screens.

PART 1 · CUSTOMER BUYING JOURNEY

Seven stages to complete a purchase

The customer follows progress in their account while the subscriber manages each business action in Buying.

  1. 01ValuationCustomer submits item details, condition and photographs.
  2. 02OfferBusiness reviews the request and sends a cash or trade-in offer.
  3. 03ShippingBusiness supplies the label or QR code and shipping instructions.
  4. 04ReceivedCustomer confirms dispatch; business confirms physical arrival.
  5. 05InspectionBusiness checks the item and records its condition.
  6. 06PaymentBusiness records payment against the agreed offer.
  7. 07CompletePurchase is completed and the item moves into stock.
CUSTOMER ACCOUNT

Track progress at every stage

The customer can see where each item has reached, including whether the business is waiting for a shipping label, receipt, inspection or payment.

WHO DOES WHAT

Customer and business actions

Customer

  1. Submit item details, condition and photographs.
  2. Choose cash, trade-in or decline the offer.
  3. Use the supplied shipping label and confirm dispatch.
  4. Review and respond if a revised offer is sent.
  5. Follow payment and completion status in the account.

Subscriber business

  1. Review the request and send cash or trade-in values.
  2. Arrange shipping with the chosen carrier outside TradeFlow.
  3. Upload the label or QR code, tracking details and instructions.
  4. Confirm receipt, inspect the item and accept, revise or refuse.
  5. Record payment and complete the purchase.
SUBSCRIBER · BUYING

Inspect the item and decide what happens next

Record the inspected condition, confirm the supplied details and choose to accept the original offer, make a counteroffer or refuse the purchase. If the original offer is unchanged, the customer does not need to accept it again. A revised offer must be presented for the customer to accept or decline.

Important: TradeFlow does not book couriers, collect shipping payments or automatically pay customers. Shipping is arranged by the subscriber outside TradeFlow. Payment is made by the subscriber and recorded in the platform.

PART 2 · AFTER THE PURCHASE

Purchased item → retail sale

After completion, the item becomes business stock. The subscriber prepares its retail listing and publishes it to the customer-facing shop.

  1. InventoryPurchased item and inspection details are retained.
  2. Prepare listingCheck details, condition, photographs and asking price.
  3. Publish to shopMake the listing available on the subscriber website.
  4. Sale & fulfilmentComplete the customer order and dispatch.
SUBSCRIBER · INVENTORY

Move the purchased item into stock

The completed purchase appears in Inventory, where the business can review its status and continue preparing it for sale.

CUSTOMER-FACING WEBSITE

Customers can browse the published shop

Published products appear in the subscriber’s retail shop, ready for customers to view and purchase.
